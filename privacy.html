Privacy Policy

How VideoCall handles your information.

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, VideoCall may collect account information such as your name, email address and phone number, together with information needed to provide authentication, messaging and calling features.

2. Account information

Your email is used for authentication and account communication. Your phone number may be used to help you find or add contacts when that feature is enabled. We do not intend to publish a public directory of users.

3. Messages and conversations

Messages and conversation metadata may be stored in our database so the messaging service can work across your devices. Access is controlled through database security policies and authenticated sessions.

4. Voice and video

When you start a call, your browser or device may request access to your microphone and camera. Audio and video are used to provide the calling experience. You can deny these permissions through your browser or device settings.

5. Contacts

VideoCall is designed to allow users to search for another registered user by phone number when adding a contact. We will not access your device address book unless a future feature explicitly requests permission and you grant it.

6. Authentication and security

We use authentication and database security controls to protect accounts and data. Authentication may use email/password and, when configured, email verification codes. Never share your password or verification code with anyone.

7. Service providers

VideoCall may use third-party infrastructure providers such as Supabase for authentication/database services, Render for application hosting, and email providers such as Resend for transactional email. These providers process information only as necessary to provide their services.

8. Cookies and local storage

The application may use browser storage and necessary cookies or similar technologies to maintain authentication sessions and application preferences.

9. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet security requirements, resolve disputes and comply with applicable obligations. Retention periods may vary by data type.

10. Your choices

You may control camera and microphone permissions through your device or browser. You may also request account assistance or deletion through the support mechanism provided by the application.

11. Children's privacy

VideoCall is not intended for children who are not permitted to use online communication services under applicable law. Do not create an account if you are not legally permitted to do so.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the application develops. The updated version will be published on this page with a new update date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the support/contact method provided by VideoCall.
